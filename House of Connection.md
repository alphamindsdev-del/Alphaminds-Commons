# **House of Connection**

## **Mission**

To help people build healthy, meaningful, and lasting relationships
through intentional connection, effective communication, mutual respect,
and a strong sense of belonging.

The House of Connection is dedicated to strengthening the relationships
that shape our lives---whether romantic, family-centered, or
intentionally single. It is a place where members learn to connect with
others authentically while building communities rooted in trust,
empathy, and shared growth.

## **Vision**

To cultivate a global community where people experience deeper
relationships, stronger families, healthier partnerships, and fulfilling
lives through genuine human connection.

## **Core Values**

-   Authenticity

-   Respect

-   Trust

-   Empathy

-   Healthy Communication

-   Belonging

-   Mutual Growth
