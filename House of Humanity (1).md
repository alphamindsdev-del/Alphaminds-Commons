# **House of Humanity 💚**

## **Mission**

To inspire members to improve the lives of others through service,
mentorship, compassion, and meaningful action.

The House of Humanity is dedicated to transforming empathy into impact
by creating opportunities for members to serve communities, mentor
others, and contribute to projects that improve society.

## **Vision**

To build a global community of compassionate individuals who believe
that a meaningful life is measured not only by personal success, but by
the positive difference they make in the lives of others.

## **Core Values**

-   Compassion

-   Service

-   Integrity

-   Responsibility

-   Generosity

-   Collaboration

-   Community Impact
