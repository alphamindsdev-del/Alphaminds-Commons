## **Looking ahead - Later**

As AlphaMinds grows, we could add specialty wellness communities without changing the House itself, such as:

- 🚶 Walk Club
- 🏊 Swim Club
- 🚴 Cycle Club
- 🧘 Yoga & Stretch Room
- 😴 Sleep Better Room
- 👩‍🍳 Healthy Cooking Room
- 💪 Strength Lab
- 🩺 Chronic Conditions Room
- 🦴 Healthy Ageing Room
- 🧬 Longevity Room

This gives the House room to evolve while keeping the initial MVP clean and easy to navigate. My recommendation for MVP 1 is to launch with the five core rooms-**BeatLift, Nutrition, Recovery, MindWell, and Vitality**-because together they cover the major dimensions of wellness without overwhelming new members.
