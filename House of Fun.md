# **House of Fun 🎭**

To create joyful experiences that strengthen wellbeing, build meaningful
relationships, inspire creativity, and remind us that flourishing
includes laughter, adventure, and play.

## **Vision**

To make fun intentional, inclusive, meaningful, and transformative.

## **Core Values**

-   Joy is essential.

-   Play builds connection.

-   Creativity expands the mind.

-   Laughter heals.

-   Shared experiences create lasting memories.

-   Fun should leave people better than it found them.

# **Recommendation for MVP 1**

Keep only **three Rooms**.

🎭 **House of Fun**

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Room**                            **Focus**
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  🎵 Rhythm & Soul Room               Music, dance and creative
                                      expression

  🎲 Games Room                       Social games, debates and
                                      intellectual play

  🌍 Adventure Room                   Outdoor experiences, travel and
                                      exploration
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

This gives you three distinct experiences:

-   **Express** (Rhythm & Soul)

-   **Play** (Games)

-   **Explore** (Adventure)

They complement one another without overlapping.

## **[A suggestion about the Game Room]{.mark}**

I would avoid making the Room revolve around specific games. Instead,
make the Room about **categories** of games. Individual games can come
and go over time.

**For example:**

### **🧠 Critical Thinking Games**

-   Rel-Fi

-   Bullshit Detector

-   Myth Busters

-   Fact or Fiction

### **🎤 Debate Games**

-   Clash of Religions

-   Clash of Ideas

-   Red vs Blue

-   Ethical Dilemmas

### **🎲 Social Games**

-   Truth or Myth

-   Two Truths and a Lie

-   Would You Rather

-   Guess the Story

### **🏆 Team Challenges**

-   Trivia League

-   Escape Challenge

-   Alpha Olympics

-   House vs House Competitions

This approach lets the Games Room grow continuously without needing to
redesign the app every time you invent a new game. Instead, you simply
add a new game to the existing library, keeping the Room fresh and
giving members reasons to return.
